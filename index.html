Package 1 Detailed Itinerary (3-Day Schedule)

Day 1: First, you are landing at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport. Our shuttle is taking you directly to your 4-star hotel for express check-in.

Day 2: Subsequently, our travel guide will lead an insightful tour around the Sydney Opera House and the historic Rocks district.

Day 3: Finally, you are visiting Bondi Beach for leisure before your evening departure.

Package 2 Detailed Itinerary (4-Day Schedule)

Day 1: First, we are departing Sydney via the scenic Grand Pacific Drive towards Jervis Bay.

Day 2: After that, you are checking into Booderee Eco-Lodge. In the afternoon, you could relax at Hyams Beach.

Day 3: In the evening, our marine specialist is leading our signature night walk to observe bioluminescent waters in Blenheim Beach.

Day 4: Finally, our coach is transporting guests back to Sydney Airport.

Package 3 Detailed Itinerary (6-Day Schedule)

Days 1-2: First, you will stay two nights in Sydney, taking guided architectural tours and scenic bay ferry rides.

Days 3-4: Subsequently, we are travelling to Jervis Bay for an open catamaran cruise to encounter resident dolphins and seabirds.

Days 5-6: Finally, you will explore the glowing coast at night and conclude with an exclusive coastal dinner.
