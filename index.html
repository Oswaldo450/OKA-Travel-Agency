Package 1 Detailed Itinerary (Express Sydney)

Day 1: Arrival & Harbour Check-in

First, you are landing at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport. Our OKA shuttle is meeting you at the terminal to transfer you directly to the Harbour Grand Hotel without delays.

Day 2: Opera House & City Discovery

Subsequently, our certified bilingual guide will lead you through the Sydney Opera House and the historic Rocks district. In the evening, you will enjoy a complimentary harbor dinner.

Day 3: Coastal Bondi Walk & Departure

Finally, you are taking the scenic Bondi to Coogee coastal walk. Later in the afternoon, our agency is coordinating your return airport shuttle.

Package 2 Detailed Itinerary (Jervis Bay Glow)

Day 1: Scenic Drive to Jervis Bay

First, our private coach is picking you up in Sydney to travel south along the Grand Pacific Drive. You are checking into Booderee Eco-Lodge surrounded by local wildlife.

Day 2: Hyams Beach & Forest Exploration

After that, you are visiting Hyams Beach, renowned for having the whitest sand in the world. You could enjoy a picnic since the afternoon schedule is flexible.

Day 3: The Bioluminescent Wonder

Subsequently, when darkness falls, our marine specialist is guiding you to Blenheim Beach. If the water is disturbed, the microscopic algae will emit a turquoise glow across the shore.

Day 4: Farewell & Sydney Transfer

Finally, after breakfast, we are returning to Sydney for your flight home.

Package 3 Detailed Itinerary (Grand Explorer)

Days 1 - 2: Sydney Landmarks & Gastronomy

First, you will stay two nights in luxury Sydney accommodation experiencing private city highlights, bridge climbs, and fine dining.

Days 3 - 4: Jervis Bay Wildlife Cruise

Subsequently, we are transferring our group to Jervis Bay Marine Park for a catamaran cruise where you will spot bottlenose dolphins and fur seals.

Days 5 - 6: Glowing Sands & National Reserve

Finally, you will witness the glowing bioluminescent beach at night, followed by a guided tour through Booderee National Park before our coach departs.
